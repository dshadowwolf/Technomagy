## Artwork

**NightWindImmortal** created the Mana Redirector artwork used on the Node Director.
Her textures include the neutral platform, all eight compass direction variants,
and the illuminated and unlit side panels:

- `assets/technomagy/textures/block/mana_redirector_top.png`
- `assets/technomagy/textures/block/mana_redirector_{north,north_east,east,south_east,south,south_west,west,north_west}.png`
- `assets/technomagy/textures/block/mana_redirector_side_on.png`
- `assets/technomagy/textures/block/mana_redirector_side_off.png`

The game-ready versions align the direction lamps and use a 64×64 canvas while
preserving her original pixel-art design and palette.
